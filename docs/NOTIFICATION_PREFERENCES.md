# Notification Preferences

User preferences live in `notification_preferences`. Category-level overrides live in `notification_category_preferences`.

Preferences control in-app, email, future SMS, future push, quiet hours, notification language, and digest mode.

Critical notifications cannot be fully silenced. They create at least an in-app delivery so booking safety, host-unresponsive, deposit, cancellation, and dispute events remain visible.

Quiet hours can delay low and normal notifications. Urgent and critical notifications may bypass quiet hours according to priority and user settings.
