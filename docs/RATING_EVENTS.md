# Rating Events

`rating_events` record confirmed signals that may influence reputation. Reviews create confirmed review events. Confirmed complaints, confirmed mismatches, accepted deposit deductions, and confirmed no-shows can create negative events.

Unconfirmed complaints, open disputes, draft complaints, unconfirmed mismatch reports, unconfirmed damage claims, and unconfirmed no-shows do not affect rating automatically.

Open disputes can freeze rating impact. Rejected claims should mark related events ignored so they no longer count.
