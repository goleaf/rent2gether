# Complaint Rating Impact

Unconfirmed complaints do not automatically affect guest or host ratings.

Rating impact can be recorded only when:

- the complaint is confirmed or partially confirmed;
- a dispute is resolved against a party;
- objective evidence supports the issue;
- a party accepted responsibility.

Rejected complaints, cancelled complaints, unsupported claims, and open disputes must not lower ratings automatically.

Resolved complaints can record problem-resolution quality separately from blame. A quick, fair response should reduce negative impact and may improve a future problem-resolution metric.
