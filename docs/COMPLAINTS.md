# Complaints

Complaints are structured problem reports from a guest, host, or system process. A complaint is always stored with the most specific available context: booking, stay, check-in, checkout, property, room, sleeping place, reporter, and the party or object the complaint is against.

Complaints are not disputes by default. If the other party accepts the problem and a resolution is completed, the complaint can close without opening a dispute.

The main flow is:

1. Reporter submits a complaint with type, severity, description, and desired resolution.
2. The system stores booking and sleeping-place context.
3. Parties are created and the other party is notified.
4. Evidence and responses are collected.
5. A resolution can create cleaning, maintenance, relocation, cancellation, refund, deposit, or dispute flows.
6. The complaint closes only after the outcome is recorded.

Emergency complaints may be submitted without evidence so the person can act quickly. The UI must still encourage evidence later and recommend local emergency services when there is immediate danger.
