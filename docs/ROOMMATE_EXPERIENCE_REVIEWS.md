# Roommate Experience Reviews

Roommate feedback is stored in `roommate_experience_reviews` and linked to the parent review, booking, property, room, and sleeping place.

Public UI must show roommate experience as an aggregate or safe summary. It must not expose full names, phone numbers, personal details, or specific accusations about current or past residents.

This data can support compatibility signals such as quietness, cleanliness, friendliness, and conflict frequency.
