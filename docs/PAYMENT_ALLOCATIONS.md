# Payment Allocations

Payment allocations explain what a payment covers.

Example for a 126 EUR payment:

- accommodation: 60 EUR
- cleaning fee: 10 EUR
- guest service fee: 6 EUR
- deposit: 50 EUR

Deposits are marked refundable. Guest service fees are non-refundable by default.

Allocation types:

- `accommodation`
- `cleaning_fee`
- `guest_service_fee`
- `deposit`
- `tax_future`
- `city_fee_future`
- `extra_guest_fee`
- `early_check_in_fee`
- `late_checkout_fee`
- `extension_amount`
- `relocation_difference`
- `other`
