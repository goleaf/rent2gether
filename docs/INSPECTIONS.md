# Inspections

Inspection tasks verify whether a place can safely and honestly be used by the next guest.

Canonical tables:

- `inspection_tasks`
- `inspection_task_items`
- `inspection_task_media`
- `inspection_status_logs`
- `inspection_events`

Inspections can be created after checkout, after cleaning, before check-in, after repair, after complaint, for deposit review, inventory checks, same-day turnover checks, safety checks, or manually.

Passing an inspection can release the calendar block. Failed inspections can require cleaning, repair, deposit review, and continued calendar blocking.
