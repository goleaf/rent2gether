# Inventory Privacy

Hosts can see inventory for their own properties, rooms, sleeping places, and bookings.

Guests can see:

- items issued to them;
- whether a return is expected;
- safe public item information promised in the listing;
- issue notices that relate to their booking.

Guests must not see:

- purchase price;
- supplier data;
- internal host notes;
- private operational contacts;
- inventory history from other guests;
- internal or future-review media.

`InventoryPrivacyService` filters item, assignment, issue, and media visibility.
