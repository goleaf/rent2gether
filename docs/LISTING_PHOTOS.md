# Listing Photos

Photos exist on three levels:

- `property_photos`
- `room_photos`
- `sleeping_place_photos`

Each photo has visibility:

- `public`
- `after_booking`
- `host_only`

At least one public sleeping place photo is required before publishing. Photos that show keys, door codes, access instructions, or private personal details should not be public.

Photo services enforce host ownership and store metadata without putting upload or visibility logic in Blade.
