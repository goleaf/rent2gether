# Complaint Resolutions

Resolution options are proposed actions attached to a complaint. They do not automatically move money or change calendar/search state until accepted or explicitly applied by a service.

Supported resolution types include explanation, apology, fix, cleaning, repair, replace item, provide missing item, relocation, cancellation, partial or full refund, deposit return or deduction, compensation, future warnings, dispute, and close without action.

If both sides agree, the resolution can be accepted and converted into the relevant action:

- cleaning creates a host cleaning task;
- repair creates a future maintenance link;
- relocation creates a booking relocation;
- cancellation creates a complaint-related cancellation preview/case;
- refund creates a booking refund linked to the complaint;
- deposit creates a future deposit case link.

If the reporter rejects the resolution or money/deposit/refund remains contested, a dispute can be opened.
