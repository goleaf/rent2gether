# Cancellation After Check-In

After check-in cancellation is treated as early termination. The service calculates nights used and nights unused, keeps the calendar blocked, and prepares refund amounts for unused stay value when the policy or cancellation reason allows it.

This module does not replace checkout. Checkout, inspection, cleaning, deposit review, and repair handling remain responsible for deciding when the sleeping place can be opened again.

Housing-problem, listing-mismatch, and host-unresponsive cancellations can mark the cancellation as dispute-ready or complaint-ready without creating support staff workflows.
