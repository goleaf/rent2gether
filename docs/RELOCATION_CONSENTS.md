# Relocation Consents

Relocation generally requires explicit consent because it changes the guest's lived place.

## Guest Consent Is Required When

- the sleeping place changes;
- the room or property changes;
- rules or privacy change;
- there is an additional payment;
- the host proposed the relocation.

## Host Consent Is Required When

- the guest requested relocation;
- the host must prepare and block another place;
- price difference, refund, or no-extra-charge handling is needed;
- the old place must stay blocked for cleaning, inspection, repair, or complaint handling.

## Rules

- Do not move a guest to a worse place without consent.
- Do not require payment without guest consent.
- Store consent status in `booking_relocation_consents`.
- Record consent events in `booking_relocation_events`.
