# Complaint Privacy

Complaint and dispute privacy is participant-scoped.

Guests can see complaints where they are reporter, affected guest, against party, or named complaint party. Hosts can see complaints tied to their bookings or listings. Roommate details and witness details must not be exposed unless explicitly allowed by the evidence or party visibility.

Hidden fields:

- `future_review_required`
- `future_review_comment`
- `future_decision_required`
- `future_decision_comment`
- internal evidence
- future-review-only evidence
- private payment/provider payloads

Normal guest and host UI must not expose internal review fields and must not create support, moderator, admin, or staff panels.
